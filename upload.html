Tips for best results
01
Anonymise before uploading. Replace employee names with anonymous IDs (EMP001, EMP002…). The calculations do not require real names and the Privacy Policy strongly recommends anonymisation.
02
Use one currency throughout. The platform processes one currency per upload. If your org spans multiple currencies, upload the primary entity first.
03
Perf and Potential are 1–5 scales. Performance 1 = far below expectations, 5 = exceptional. Potential 1 = limited, 5 = high. The platform derives placement (Star, High Performer, HiPo, Core, Inconsistent) from these two scores.
04
Grade naming matters. Use one of: Junior, Mid, Senior, Lead, Principal, Director, VP, C-Suite. These drive the grade-level performance benchmarks in L2 Talent Premium.
05
Your data disappears when you close the tab. This is by design. For repeated use, keep your anonymised CSV file saved locally and re-upload each session. No data is ever stored on our servers.
