The five pillars

HR's complete
financial language.

Each pillar maps a dimension of human capital to a financial statement line. Together they produce your organisation's first Human P&L.

L1
Value Ledger
Every people decision logged as a financial event. Hire, develop, lose, or mismanage a person — it goes into the Value Ledger with a rupee figure attached. The foundation of the Human P&L. What HR does becomes what HR costs and creates, in numbers your CFO can audit.
OUTPUT → Human transaction log with INR values · Monthly closing entry · Year-on-year comparison
L2
Talent Premium
The financial premium your talent generates above market average. Measures what exceptional people actually return versus what average performers cost — and what it costs when you lose them.
OUTPUT → Talent premium per role · Replacement cost exposure · Retention ROI
L3
Org Vitals
The financial health indicators of your organisation's people system. Attrition cost, engagement yield, absence impact, and productivity variance — translated into a single quarterly Org Vitals report.
OUTPUT → Quarterly Org Vitals report · Leading indicators · Board-ready summary
L4
Human P&L
The complete income statement for your people function. Revenue contribution from people decisions. Cost of people failures. Net human margin. For the first time, HR gets a P&L — and a seat at the table that comes with it.
OUTPUT → Monthly Human P&L · Human Gross Margin · HR contribution to EBITDA
L5
Net Human Worth
The balance sheet entry for your organisation's human capital. Total asset value of your workforce, net of attrition risk, culture degradation, and capability gaps. The number that belongs in your annual report.
OUTPUT → Human capital balance sheet entry · Net Human Worth · Year-end valuation
