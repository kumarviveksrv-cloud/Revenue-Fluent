You work with multiple organisations. You need a credible, transferable framework for client conversations.

You need the language and numbers to present HR as an investment, not a cost centre.
Attrition — understanding and quantifying the real cost
You know attrition is expensive. You need the number, not the estimate.
Talent quality — identifying and retaining the people who matter most
You need to know which people decisions are delivering and which are leaking value.
Culture and org health — diagnosing what is really happening
You sense the org is not operating at its potential. You need a precise diagnosis, not a survey.
HR strategy — building a financially grounded people strategy
You are building or rebuilding the HR function and want it to be financially literate from the start.
